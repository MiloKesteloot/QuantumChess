Moves

    How to play

    Checkers on an 8×8 board, plus superposition. Men move diagonally forward and become kings on the far row. Jumping is optional, and you can stop a multi-jump after any hop.

    Splitting

    Instead of one move, a piece can make two moves at once and end up in two places. The game now tracks two possible worlds, one for each choice. A piece can be in at most 4 places.

    Collapse

    Every move must be a legal, ordinary checkers move in each world it applies to. Worlds where it isn't are deleted. That's how pieces collapse:

    • Jumping a ghost proves it was really there. It's captured, and its other ghosts vanish.
    • Moving onto a ghost's square proves it wasn't there.
    • Moving a superposed piece: each ghost can move (or split again). Any ghost you don't move is ruled out, since every world needs its piece to move.

    Winning

    You lose when you have no legal move in any world. Draw after 40 moves each of king-only shuffling in every world, or by agreement.

    The guarantee

    Every surviving world is a normal game of checkers where each turn one piece made one legal move. Open Timelines at any point to step through one.

    Controls

    • Click a piece, then a destination. Click the destination again to play the move.
    • To split, click a second destination before confirming, then click either destination again.
    • For a multi-jump, click each landing square in turn, then click the last one again.
    • For a piece that's already split, click each ghost and give it a destination. Ghosts you don't move are ruled out.
    • The panel shows what your move will collapse before you confirm. Enter also plays it, Backspace takes back a step, Esc clears. Hold Preview to see the result.
    • Hover (or tap) a ghost to highlight its other ghosts and see what else would be true if it's the real one. A ♛? crown means it's a king in only some worlds.
